Canyon Clash

Take turns throwing across the canyon. Five body hits or two headshots and you’re out.

Player 1, pick your hero

Player 2, pick your hero

Drag back anywhere on the arena like a slingshot, then let go. Keyboard: ↑ ↓ angle, ← → power, Space to throw.

Hit a floating bubble mid-flight to grab it: green heals 20 HP, orange doubles your next throw’s damage, blue blocks the next hit on you.
